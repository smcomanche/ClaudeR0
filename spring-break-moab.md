## Day 3 — Sunday, March 29: Full Trail Day

### Morning Ride: Klonzo Trailhead
- **Address:** Willow Springs Trail, Moab, UT 84532
- **Trailforks:** [trailforks.com/region/klonzo](https://www.trailforks.com/region/klonzo/)

## Day 4 — Monday, March 30: Arches + Dead Horse Point

### Morning: Arches National Park
- **Website:** [nps.gov/arch](https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/hours.htm)
- **Address:** Moab, UT 84532
- **Hours:** Visitor Center 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM
- **Details:** No timed entry required in 2026 — but get there early to beat entrance lines and grab parking at popular trailheads (especially weekends). Drive the scenic road, hit the Windows section, and hike to **Delicate Arch** (3 miles round trip, 480 ft elevation gain — the iconic Utah arch). Balanced Rock is a quick roadside stop. Bring water and snacks.
- [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?cid=10985816749763642722)

### Afternoon Ride: Dead Horse Point — Intrepid Trail System
- **Website:** [stateparks.utah.gov/parks/dead-horse/intrepid-trail](https://stateparks.utah.gov/parks/dead-horse/intrepid-trail/)
- **Address:** Dead Horse Point State Park, Moab, UT 84532
- **Hours:** 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM | Entry fee: $20/vehicle
- **Details:** 16.6 miles of singletrack through juniper and pinyon above spectacular canyons. Mix of beginner and intermediate trails — perfect for the family. Jaw-dropping canyon overlooks. The views alone are worth the drive. Susan can run or gravel bike the paved park roads.
- [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?cid=17161838762983602508)

### Dinner: Antica Forma

## Key Info & Tips

- **Spring weather in Moab (late March):** Highs 60–70°F, lows 35–45°F. Layers are essential. Sun is intense even when cool.
- **Hydration:** Desert riding is no joke. Bring more water than you think. 2+ liters per person per ride minimum.
- **Sand Flats entry fee:** $10/vehicle for Slickrock area
- **Dead Horse Point entry fee:** $20/vehicle
- **Arches National Park:** No timed entry required in 2026. Arrive early to avoid entrance lines and parking issues at popular spots.
- **Moab trail ratings are soft:** What's rated intermediate in Moab would often be advanced elsewhere. Start easier and work up.
- **Bike shops:** Chile Pepper Bike Shop and Poison Spider Bicycles are both on Main St for any mechanical needs or last-minute gear.
