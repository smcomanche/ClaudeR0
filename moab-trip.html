Day 4 — Monday, March 30

Arches National Park + Dead Horse Point
▼
Morning

🏜️ Arches National Park

No timed entry required in 2026 — arrive early for parking. Drive the scenic road, hit the Windows section, and hike to Delicate Arch (3 mi RT, 480 ft gain — the iconic Utah arch). Balanced Rock is a quick roadside stop.
📍 Arches National Park, Moab, UT 84532 🕐 Visitor Center 9 AM – 4 PM 🔗 NPS Info
Delicate Arch Hike:
  • 3 miles round trip, 480 ft elevation gain
  • The iconic Utah arch — must-do
  • Bring water and snacks
  • Arrive early to beat parking
Afternoon Ride

🚵 Dead Horse Point — Intrepid Trail System

16.6 miles of singletrack through juniper and pinyon above spectacular canyons. Mix of beginner and intermediate trails. Jaw-dropping canyon overlooks. Susan can run or gravel bike the paved park roads.
📍 Dead Horse Point State Park, Moab, UT 84532 🕐 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM 💵 $20/vehicle 🔗 Trail Info

💡 Key Tips

▼
🌡️
Spring Weather Highs 60–70°F, lows 35–45°F. Layers are essential. Sun is intense even when cool.
💧
Hydration Desert riding is no joke. 2+ liters per person per ride minimum. Bring more than you think.
💵
Fees Sand Flats (Slickrock): $10/vehicle
Dead Horse Point: $20/vehicle
🏜️
Arches — No Timed Entry No timed entry required in 2026. Arrive early to avoid entrance lines and parking at popular spots.
⚠️
Trail Ratings Moab trail ratings are soft — what's rated intermediate here would often be advanced elsewhere. Start easier and work up.
🔧
Bike Shops Chile Pepper Bike Shop and Poison Spider Bicycles are both on Main St for mechanicals or last-minute gear.
